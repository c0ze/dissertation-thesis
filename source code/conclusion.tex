\section{Conclusion}

As long as Quantum computers with a sufficient memory to apply some useful functions for some basic tasks are still under development, Quantum Simulators will provide the possibility for scientists to research new algorithms.
The difficulties encountered in simulating Quantum Computers with Classical Computers make us develop new methods. Parallel Computation is one of the most promising ways to simulate a Quantum Computer and recently many researchers are interested in this subject.

In this thesis, the basics of Quantum Computation and some strategies for a parallel simulation have been discussed, and one of them has been implemented. Due to time limitations, the library could not be developed fully, many functions to provide basic Quantum Computer operations (ie Quantum Fourrier Transform) was unfinished.
A complete library could prove quite useful for research into Quantum Computation algorithms.
