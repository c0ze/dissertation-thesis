\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
This thesis gives a brief introduction to {\tt Quantum Computation} and discusses implementation schemas for a {\tt Quantum Computer} simulator on a distributed system.  
Research into {\tt Quantum Computers} have increased drastically since Peter Shor showed that some problems which can only be solved in exponential time with classical computers can be solved in polynomial time with quantum computers. Other algorithms have also been discovered where {\tt Quantum Computers} have an advantage over their classical counterparts. There are still great difficulties in physical realization of {\tt Quantum Computers}, and so far machines with only a few bits of memory have been accomplished. Because of this, much of the research into {\tt Quantum Computation} algorithms make use of simulators. Due to the fundamental differences between Classical Computers and {\tt Quantum Computers} it is quite inefficient to simulate a {\tt Quantum Computer} with a reasonable memory. This thesis investigates the possible strategies for simulating a {\tt Quantum Computer} on a parallel Linux cluster.
